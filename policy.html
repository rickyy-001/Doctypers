Client payment and project policy

How we work with clients: what we charge, when, and what to expect along the way.

  • 50% Deposit before work starts
  • 50% Due on completion, before handover
  • 2 Rounds of revisions included
  • MoMo Mobile Money or bank transfer

Payment structure

All projects require a 50% deposit upfront before work begins, with the remaining 50% due on completion, before the final files or live site access are handed over.

  • Deposit (50%): due before any work starts
  • Final payment (50%): due before final handover or launch
  • Accepted payment methods: Mobile Money, bank transfer

Project timeline

Each project will have an agreed start date and estimated completion date, confirmed in writing (chat) before work begins.

Delays caused by the client, such as late feedback, missing content or slow responses, will push the delivery date back accordingly and are not our responsibility.

Revisions

Each project includes 2 rounds of revisions at no extra cost, to adjust layout, text, colours or minor design elements within the agreed scope.

Additional revision rounds, or changes that go beyond small tweaks, will be billed separately at an hourly or flat rate agreed beforehand.

Scope changes

If a client requests new pages, features or functionality not included in the original agreement, this counts as a scope change. Scope changes require a separate quote and, where significant, an additional deposit before work on the new items begins.

Cancellation policy

If a client cancels a project after work has started:

  • The deposit is non-refundable, as it covers time already committed.
  • Any additional work completed beyond the deposit amount will be invoiced based on progress made, and is due before any files are released.

Ownership and delivery

Full ownership of the final website (files, code) transfers to the client only after the final payment has been received in full. Work-in-progress files remain our property until that point.

Communication

Client updates and feedback should go through one agreed channel (for example WhatsApp) to avoid miscommunication. A reasonable response time (for example within 24 to 48 hours) should be expected from both sides to keep the project on schedule.

Late payment

If final payment is not made within 7 days of project completion, site access or files may be withheld until payment is settled. For long overdue payments, a late fee may apply.

Questions about any of this? Get in touch before we start.
